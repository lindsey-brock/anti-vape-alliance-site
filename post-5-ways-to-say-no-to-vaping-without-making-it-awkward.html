5 Ways to Say No to Vaping (Without Making It Awkward)

November 8, 2026

There’s more than one way to say no. Pick the one that feels most like you, and practice it so it’s ready when you need it.

  • Say it simply. “No thanks. I’m not interested.” You don’t owe anyone a long explanation.
  • Give a reason. “No thanks. I want to keep my lungs healthy for soccer.”
  • Change the subject. “Nah. Let’s go shoot some hoops instead.”
  • Walk away. “I’m going to go find my other friends.” Leaving is a smart move, not a weak one.
  • Get help. “I’m uncomfortable. I’m going to tell an adult.” Asking a trusted adult for help is a sign of strength.

Remember: Stop. Think. Choose.

Take a moment, ask yourself whether it’s safe and matches your values, then make the decision that’s right for you. Real friends will respect it.

You never have to vape to fit in. If you or a friend want help quitting, This is Quitting and Smokefree Teen have free, confidential support.
