Students leading the conversation about vaping.

The Anti-Vape Alliance (AVA) is a student-founded and student-led nonprofit organization empowering young people with the facts, confidence and peer support they need to make healthy choices about vaping and nicotine.

A movement created by young people.

AVA began with students who saw a need for honest, age-appropriate vaping education before kids reach middle school. Students help shape our programs, outreach, presentations and resources.

Our goal: give students a voice in prevention — and give younger students the tools to make informed choices.

Our Mission

Peer-led prevention starts with giving students a voice.

AVA brings high-school students together to educate and mentor younger students through classroom presentations, free curriculum, school clubs and community partnerships.

01

Nonprofit Mission

Our focus is education and prevention — making useful resources available to students, teachers, parents and communities.

The AVA Difference

Peer-to-peer education can make prevention personal.

“We want younger students to hear about vaping from people they can relate to — students who understand what middle school is really like.”

AVA's student-led approach is designed to complement educators, parents, school counselors and community health professionals — not replace them.

Programs

Built by students. Designed to help schools.

Peer-Led Classroom Talks

Age-appropriate presentations for elementary and middle-school students covering vaping, nicotine, peer pressure, decision-making and healthy alternatives.

School Chapters

Students can build AVA clubs that connect high-school mentors with younger students and create ongoing prevention activities.

Community Education

AVA works with PTAs, educators, families and community organizations to expand youth-centered vaping education.
